Testing the Tribute Extension

This is a demo page to test the Tribute browser extension. The extension should inject a floating "Tip with Tribute" button in the bottom-right corner of the page, as well as inline tip buttons near content sections.

When you click on the Tribute button, you'll be able to send tips using sBTC on Stacks, with advanced features like revenue splitting, yield generation, and on-chain reputation.

What is Programmable Patronage?

Tribute transforms simple Bitcoin tips into sophisticated smart contracts. Instead of just sending money from A to B, you can:

  • Automatically split revenue among multiple recipients
  • Generate yield by staking a portion of tips
  • Build reputation on-chain for both patrons and creators

This is the future of the creator economy—powered by Bitcoin and Stacks.

How It Works

Tribute leverages sBTC on the Stacks blockchain to bring smart contract capabilities to Bitcoin. When you send a tip through Tribute, you're not just transferring value— you're executing a programmable transaction that can include:

  1. Multiple beneficiaries with custom split percentages
  2. Automatic staking for passive income
  3. Reputation scoring that builds your on-chain profile
